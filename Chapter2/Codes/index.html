Genome modification
  • bins_removal_from_fna.py
    Removes rows touching mononucleotide tracks from a genome configurational-trait CSV, producing a track-free CSV.
  • loci_removal_from_fna.py
    Removes predefined fragments (e.g. mononucleotide tracks) from a reference genome .fna file, given a CSV of chromosome/start/end coordinates.
Permutational ensemble
  • permutational_ensemble_generation.py
    Generates the full permutational ensemble of all possible DNA n-mer combinations (A/T/G/C) as a CSV.
  • permutational_ensemble_GCcontent_transform.py
    Randomly mutates a permutational ensemble of 10-mers to hit a user-defined target GC content.
  • permutational_ensemble_expansion_under_constraints.py
    Expands the permutational ensemble into a realistic DNA-like dataset meeting empirical constraints on GC content and A-/T-/AT-track proportions.
Scedasticity
  • scedascity_complexity.py
    Subsamples cumulative complexity values and averages them over multiple fragment lengths across a reference genome.
  • scedascity_thermodynamics.py
    Randomly samples genomic locations and computes GC content and thermodynamic properties (dG, dH, dS) around each one.
